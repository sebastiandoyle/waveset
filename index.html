How to use it

Three steps.
Two are easy.

Wet

Straight out of the surf or the shower. Don't towel it to death.

Scoop

One scoop, rubbed between your palms, then scrunched up through the ends.

Leave it

The hard part. No brush, no touching, no checking the mirror every ten minutes.
